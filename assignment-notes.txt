Initial observation:
The existing implementation passes its tests but does not correctly
process corpus-a. Transaction count, account distribution, category
classification, and balances do not reconcile.



Baseline finding: IngestService processes each parsed message independently, saves every parsed result without deduplication, and derives category solely from debit/credit direction. This explains why duplicate evidence can inflate transaction counts and why MICRO/TRANSFER are currently always zero.

Parser routing: Parsers registers only HDFC and ICICI SMS parsers. EmailParser exists in the project but is not registered, so supported email messages are currently skipped.

Finding #1 — Email processing is incomplete

The corpus contains email messages, but EmailParser is unimplemented and isn't registered in Parsers. Therefore email transaction evidence is currently lost.
