Root cause: The amount parser required exactly two decimal places, so Rs.5 was skipped and a later Avl Bal amount was captured instead.
Number affected: 1 corpus message and 1 normalized transaction, identified by the UPI/WATER CAN debit on account 4821.
Rule used to identify affected messages: debit alerts whose transaction amount has an integer-only rupee value and whose old parser would fall through to a balance amount.
Fix: Parse rupee amounts with an optional decimal fraction and select the transaction amount before balance fields.
Regression test: AmountsTest asserts that Rs.5 parses as 5.00, and the full corpus pipeline/self-check remains at 257 transactions with the expected balances.
